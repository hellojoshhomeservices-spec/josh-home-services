Practical Plumbing Advice

Common Plumbing Problems and What They Can Tell You.

What Should I Do About a Leaking Tap?

A leaking tap can be caused by a worn washer, seal, fitting or another component inside the tap. The visible dripping does not always tell you exactly what needs replacing.

If a simple adjustment does not stop the leak, or the tap continues to deteriorate, the fitting may need to be inspected and repaired properly.

What Should I Do About a Blocked Drain?

A blocked drain can be caused by build-up, an obstruction or another drainage problem. A slow drain is often an early warning that water is no longer flowing normally.

If basic clearing does not resolve the problem, or the same blockage keeps returning, the cause should be investigated rather than repeatedly treating the symptom.

How Do I Know If I Have a Leaking Pipe?

Possible warning signs include unexplained dampness, water marks, damaged finishes, persistent moisture, unusual water usage or water appearing where it should not.

Concealed leaks can be more difficult to identify because the actual source may not be where the visible water appears. Professional inspection may therefore be needed.

Why Is My Water Pressure Low?

Low water pressure can have several possible causes, including a restriction, plumbing fault, supply issue or another problem affecting part of the property.

The correct repair depends on identifying the cause rather than simply treating the pressure symptom.

When Should I Call a Plumber?

Professional plumbing assistance is appropriate when a leak cannot be stopped, a blockage persists, water damage is occurring, the cause of the problem is unclear or the repair requires specialist knowledge or equipment.

Problems involving water should generally not be allowed to continue simply because the initial issue appears small. Early attention can help prevent additional property damage.

Plumbing Questions

What should I do about a leaking tap?

A leaking tap should be checked to determine whether the issue is a worn washer, seal, fitting or another plumbing component. If a simple adjustment does not solve the problem, professional repair may be appropriate.

What should I do about a blocked drain?

A slow or blocked drain should not simply be ignored. The cause may be a local blockage, build-up or a wider drainage problem. If the blockage persists or repeatedly returns, professional investigation may be required.

Possible signs include unexplained damp areas, water marks, persistent moisture, damaged finishes, unusual water usage or water appearing where it should not. Concealed leaks may require inspection to identify the source.
