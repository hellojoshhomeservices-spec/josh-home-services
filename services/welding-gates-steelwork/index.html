Common Questions

Welding, Gates & Steelwork Questions People Ask in Pretoria

Do you provide welding services in Pretoria?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides practical welding and steelwork in Pretoria for property repairs, alterations, improvements and new steelwork requirements. The type of welding depends on the material, the existing structure and what needs to be made, repaired or modified.

Do you make or repair gates?

Yes. Gate work can include repairs, alterations, replacement sections and practical steelwork for property access. For example, if a steel gate has a damaged section, worn or broken components, or needs modification to suit the property, the work can be assessed before a suitable solution is recommended.

Do you do burglar bars and security steelwork?

Security-related steelwork can form part of the service where the job is suitable. This may include practical steelwork associated with gates, access points and other property security improvements. The existing property and the required level of security need to be considered before the work is specified.

Can you repair a broken or damaged steel gate?

In suitable cases, yes. A gate does not necessarily need to be replaced simply because part of it is damaged. For example, welding may be used to repair a cracked or broken steel section, strengthen a damaged area or restore part of the gate, depending on the condition of the existing gate and its fittings.

Do you make custom steel gates?

Custom steelwork can be considered where the required gate, dimensions, opening and intended use are suitable for the project. The design needs to take into account the available space, how the gate will operate and how it connects with the existing property.

Can you weld steel that needs to be repaired?

Welding can be used for suitable steel repairs, alterations and reinforcement work. The correct approach depends on the thickness and condition of the steel, the damaged area and whether the existing steel is suitable for repair rather than replacement.

Do you do steelwork for homes?

Yes. Residential steelwork can include practical work around gates, access areas, security improvements, repairs and other property requirements. Each job is assessed according to the existing structure and what the homeowner needs the steelwork to achieve.

Do you provide welding and steelwork for businesses?

Suitable commercial property requirements can also be considered. The work may involve repairs, alterations, gates, access-related steelwork or other practical steelwork, subject to the requirements of the property and the job.

Do you do gate repairs near me in Pretoria?

Josh Home Services is based in Pretoria East and works across Pretoria and selected surrounding areas. If you need a gate repaired, modified or assessed, the location, type of gate and nature of the problem can be discussed before the work is arranged.

Can you repair steelwork instead of replacing it?

Sometimes. If the existing steel is structurally and practically suitable for repair, welding, strengthening or modification may be more appropriate than replacing the whole item. The condition of the steel and the extent of the damage need to be assessed first.

Can welding and steelwork form part of a renovation?

Yes. Steelwork can form part of a wider renovation or property improvement, particularly where gates, access, security or other steel components need to be repaired, altered or added as part of the project.
