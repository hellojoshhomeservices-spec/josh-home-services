Common Questions

Renovation Questions People Ask in Pretoria

Do you provide renovation services in Pretoria?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides renovation and property improvement work across Pretoria. Depending on the project, this can involve changes to existing rooms or areas, repairs, building-related work and other practical improvements to the property. For example, a renovation may involve updating an existing space while also dealing with damaged surfaces, plumbing requirements or other work discovered during the project.

Do you do house renovations in Pretoria?

Yes. Residential renovation work can be discussed for houses, townhouses, estates and other suitable properties in Pretoria. The scope depends on what you want changed and the condition of the existing property. For example, a renovation might involve improving an existing room, changing finishes, repairing damaged areas or carrying out several related property improvements as one project.

Can you renovate a bathroom?

Bathroom renovations can involve several different parts of the property, including existing finishes, plumbing, fittings and other practical requirements. Josh Home Services can discuss the bathroom renovation you have in mind and determine what work is required. For example, an older bathroom may need improvements to the existing space together with plumbing repairs or alterations.

Can you renovate a kitchen?

Kitchen renovation requirements can vary considerably from one property to another. A project may involve improving the existing space, repairing areas that need attention or carrying out related property work. The existing layout, surfaces, plumbing and other requirements need to be considered before deciding what work is appropriate.

Can renovations include plumbing work?

Yes. Plumbing can form part of a renovation where the project requires plumbing repairs, alterations or improvements. For example, changing a bathroom or kitchen may require plumbing work to accommodate the renovation. The plumbing requirements depend on the existing installation and what is being changed.

Can renovations include roofing or waterproofing?

Yes. Where a property renovation also involves roofing or waterproofing requirements, those issues can form part of the broader property work. For example, renovation work may reveal water ingress, damaged roof areas or another moisture-related problem that needs attention as part of the overall project.

Do you renovate old houses?

Older properties can have different renovation requirements from newer buildings because existing surfaces, structures, plumbing and other property elements may need attention before improvements are made. Josh Home Services can discuss the condition of the property and the renovation work required. The actual scope depends on what is found on the property.

Do you do small renovations and property improvements?

Renovation work does not always involve a complete property makeover. Some projects involve improving one room, repairing a specific area or carrying out several smaller improvements around the property. The suitability of the work depends on the requirements and scope of the particular project.

Do you provide commercial renovation services in Pretoria?

Yes. Suitable commercial property renovation and improvement work can be discussed for businesses and other commercial properties in Pretoria. Commercial work can involve different practical requirements from residential renovations, so the property, scope and work required need to be considered before proceeding.

How much does a renovation cost in Pretoria?

Renovation costs depend on the size of the project, the condition of the existing property, the materials and finishes required, and the amount of repair or alteration work involved. A bathroom improvement, for example, can have very different requirements from a larger house renovation. An appropriate price therefore depends on understanding the actual work required for the property.

Can you help with renovation work after buying a house?

Yes. Renovation work after buying a property is common when the new owner wants to improve, repair or update parts of the house. This could include several smaller jobs or a larger renovation project. Josh Home Services can discuss the property and the improvements you want to make.
