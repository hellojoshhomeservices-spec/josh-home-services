Common Questions

Paving, Landscaping & Rubble Removal Questions

Do you do paving in Pretoria?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides practical paving work for residential and other properties across Pretoria. This can include paving for areas such as driveways, walkways, entrances and other outdoor surfaces where a durable, practical finished area is required.

Can you pave a driveway?

Yes. Driveway paving is one of the practical applications of paving work. For example, an existing driveway area may need to be paved, repaired or improved to provide a more usable and finished surface around the property. The condition and layout of the area will affect what work is required.

Do you do paving repairs or only new paving?

Paving requirements are not always about installing a completely new area. Existing paving may have sections that have moved, become uneven or require attention as part of wider property improvements. The required work depends on the condition of the existing surface and the intended use of the area.

Do you provide landscaping services in Pretoria?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides practical landscaping and outdoor property improvements. Depending on the project, this can involve improving the layout and usability of an outdoor area, working around existing paving or carrying out other practical exterior improvements to the property.

Can you help with a garden or outdoor area that needs cleaning up and improving?

Yes. Outdoor property projects can involve more than one type of work. For example, an area may need general clean-up, removal of building or garden-related rubble, improvements to the usable outdoor space and paving or landscaping work. The work can be discussed according to what the property actually needs rather than treating every outdoor project as the same job.

Do you remove building rubble in Pretoria?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides rubble removal as part of practical property and site clean-up work. This can be relevant after building, renovation, demolition or repair work where unwanted material has been left on the property and needs to be cleared as part of completing the project.

Can you remove rubble after renovations?

Yes. Renovation work can leave behind broken materials, construction waste and other unwanted rubble. Rubble removal can be arranged as part of the property's clean-up requirements after the renovation or repair work, helping clear the affected area so that the property can be used again.

Do you remove rubble from a building site?

Rubble removal can form part of practical site clean-up where building or property work has generated unwanted material. The type and amount of rubble, access to the property and the requirements of the job need to be considered when arranging the work.

Do you provide paving, landscaping or rubble removal near me?

Josh Home Services is based in Pretoria East and works across Pretoria and selected surrounding areas. Paving, landscaping and rubble removal requirements can be discussed based on the property's location, the type of work needed and the practical requirements of the job.

Can paving and landscaping be done as part of the same property project?

Yes. These types of outdoor work can form part of the same property improvement project. For example, a property may require a new or improved paved area together with landscaping or other exterior improvements around it. Combining the work can make sense where the different parts of the outdoor area need to work together.

Can rubble removal be included when doing other property work?

Yes. Rubble removal can be particularly useful when other property work creates material that needs to be cleared. For example, renovation, repair, paving or other improvement work may leave an area with unwanted rubble that needs to be removed before the property project is considered complete.
