Common Questions

Roofing & Waterproofing Questions

Can you repair a leaking roof in Pretoria?

Yes. Roof leaks can come from damaged or displaced tiles, deteriorated roof sheets, flashing, roof joints, or other areas where water is getting into the property. Josh Home Services can assess the problem and carry out practical roof repair work to address the source of the leak.

My roof only leaks when it rains heavily. Does that still need to be repaired?

Yes. A roof that only leaks during heavy rain can still have a genuine roofing problem. For example, water may be entering around a roof joint, flashing, damaged section or another vulnerable point that is not exposed during lighter rain. Finding and addressing the source is preferable to simply dealing with the water inside the property each time it rains.

Do you repair roof tiles that are cracked or broken?

Yes. Damaged or displaced roof tiles can allow rainwater to enter the roof structure. For example, if a tile has cracked, broken or moved out of position after severe weather, the affected area can be inspected and practical repair work can be carried out.

Do you do waterproofing in Pretoria?

Yes. Waterproofing forms part of Josh Home Services' roofing and property protection work. Waterproofing can be relevant where water is entering through vulnerable areas of a property, such as roof-related surfaces, joints or other exposed areas that require protection against water ingress.

What is the difference between a roof repair and waterproofing?

A roof repair deals with a physical roofing problem, such as a damaged tile, roof sheet or flashing. Waterproofing is focused on preventing water from penetrating through a vulnerable surface or area. In some cases both may be needed. For example, repairing the damaged section of a roof may be necessary before waterproofing is applied to the relevant area.

Can you help if water is coming through the ceiling?

Yes. Water appearing on a ceiling during or after rain can indicate a roof leak or another water-ingress problem above the affected area. For example, a damp patch or water mark on the ceiling may be caused by rain entering through damaged roofing, a roof joint or another vulnerable point. The source should be investigated rather than simply treating the mark on the ceiling.

Do you repair gutters as part of roof maintenance?

Roof drainage is an important part of protecting a property from rainwater. Problems such as damaged, blocked or poorly functioning gutters can contribute to water being directed where it should not go. Where gutter-related work forms part of the roofing or property maintenance requirement, it can be assessed as part of the overall job.

Do you provide roof repairs near me in Pretoria?

Josh Home Services is based in Pretoria East and provides roofing and waterproofing services across Pretoria and selected surrounding areas. Availability depends on the location and the type of roofing or waterproofing work required.

How do I know whether my roof needs repair or replacement?

Not every roofing problem means the entire roof needs to be replaced. A localised problem, such as a damaged tile, flashing issue or leaking section, may require targeted repair work. The appropriate solution depends on the condition and extent of the roofing problem and should be assessed before deciding on larger work.

Can you inspect a roof before I arrange the repair?

Yes. An assessment can help establish what is causing the problem and what work is actually required. This is useful when, for example, a property has repeated leaks but the exact point where rainwater is entering is not obvious from inside the building.
