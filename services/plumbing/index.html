Can you fix a leaking tap?

Yes. Leaking taps are a common plumbing repair. Depending on the cause, the work may involve addressing a worn component, a faulty fitting or another problem causing the tap to leak. The exact repair depends on the condition of the tap and the surrounding plumbing.

Can you repair a leaking pipe?

Yes. Josh Home Services can assist with leaking pipe repairs as part of its plumbing service. For example, a leak may occur around a visible pipe, a plumbing connection or beneath a sink. The source of the leak needs to be assessed before the appropriate repair can be determined.

Do you unblock blocked drains?

Josh Home Services can assist with common blocked-drain problems affecting a property. For example, a basin, sink or other drain may become slow or stop draining properly because of a blockage. The appropriate approach depends on where the blockage is located and what is causing it.

Can you fix a blocked sink or basin?

Yes. A blocked sink or basin can form part of a general plumbing repair. Where water is draining slowly or not draining at all, the plumbing problem can be assessed to determine what work is required to clear or repair it.

Do you provide plumbing repairs for homes?

Yes. Josh Home Services provides plumbing repairs for residential properties across Pretoria. This can include everyday problems such as leaking taps, leaking pipes, blocked drains and other plumbing issues around the home.

Do you provide commercial plumbing services?

Yes. Plumbing work is also available for suitable commercial property requirements. The type of work can vary from a straightforward repair to plumbing maintenance or work connected to a broader property improvement.

Do you provide emergency plumbing in Pretoria?

Plumbing problems that are causing active leaks or preventing normal use of a property can require prompt attention. Contact Josh Home Services with the details of the problem so the work and urgency can be discussed before proceeding.

How much does a plumber cost in Pretoria?

Plumbing costs depend on the problem, the property and the work required. A leaking tap, blocked drain and leaking pipe can each involve different repair requirements, so an accurate price depends on understanding what needs to be done rather than applying one fixed price to every plumbing job.
